Program Tracker — UI direction prototypes

Seven visual identities for the same product, built on the same screens and the same sample data: the overview dashboard, a client page with block pulse, and the AI draft-review flow. Compare the identity, not the content — every number and word is identical across the seven.

Ledger

The current “chalk & iron” identity, promoted: warm paper, ink, hairline rules and ruled tables like a fine print logbook. Serif italic is reserved for the client's own words. Quiet, credible, grown-up.

Palette
TypeBarlow · Barlow Semi Condensed · IBM Plex Mono · Fraunces italic
Says“A serious coaching instrument.” Reads as expertise and diligence.
RiskQuiet needs great data density to impress in a first demo.

B · departure

Platform

Dark meet-day scoring world: charcoal surfaces, chalk-white primary buttons, and the IWF plate palette (red / blue / yellow / green) doing real work as the status and RPE system. Scoreboard numerals, chart-forward block pulse.

Palette
TypeBarlow · Barlow Condensed (scoreboard display) · IBM Plex Mono
Says“An elite performance lab.” Highest demo impact; strength-sport natives read the plate colors instantly.
RiskDark-first commitment; the busiest screens must stay disciplined.

C · departure

Studio

Crisp modern light SaaS: cool neutrals, one indigo accent, compact density, soft-shadow cards, workspace switcher and ⌘K search. The AI actions own the accent color, so “AI-assisted” is visible at a glance.

Palette
TypeInstrument Sans · IBM Plex Mono
Says“A modern, funded-feeling tool.” Most familiar to SaaS buyers; least to prove.
RiskFamiliarity — reads like many other products until the AI flow does the differentiating.
Open prototype C→
D · departure

Geist

Vercel's developer-infrastructure minimalism: pure white canvas, near-black ink, Geist's tight negative tracking, shadow-as-border cards instead of CSS borders, and a workflow accent trio — Develop Blue owns every AI action, Ship Red marks severity and due-today, Preview Pink marks drafts.

Palette
TypeGeist · Geist Mono (tabular numerals)
Says“Engineered software-grade polish.” Reads like a tool built by a serious product team.
RiskNearest to generic dev-tool aesthetics; the workflow-color mapping must stay disciplined to mean something.
Open prototype D→
E · departure

Dawn

Steep-style daylight analytics: fog-gray sidebar, ceramic 24px-radius cards with soft layered shadows, a display serif for page titles only, one ink pill as the single filled CTA per screen (secondaries are text links), and exactly two chromatic data voices — rust on an Apricot wash, blue on a Sky wash. Tables become divider-free stat rows.

Palette
TypeSource Serif 4 (titles & data numbers) · Inter (UI, tabular numerals)
Says“Calm, editorial, confident.” The premium-analytics feel — friendly where Ledger is austere.
RiskSoftness — big radii and pastels must stay disciplined or the tool reads less serious.
Open prototype E→
F · departure

Onyx

Linear's engineering-dark system: a near-black blue-cast floor (#010102), hierarchy carried by a four-step surface ladder and hairlines instead of shadows, and exactly one chromatic accent — lavender-blue #5e6ad2 — reserved for the brand mark, focus rings, inline links, and a single primary AI action per screen. Compact density, Inter with scaled negative tracking, JetBrains Mono figures.

Palette
TypeInter (scaled negative tracking) · JetBrains Mono
Says“A precision tool from a serious product team.” Quiet, dense, keyboard-first.
RiskDark-first and quiet — demo impact depends on the data doing the talking. Closest sibling to D, differing in mood more than structure.
Open prototype F→
G · departure

Primer

GitHub's Primer system: content-first chrome — visible 1px #d0d7de borders, flat surfaces, no shadows — gray-bordered boxes with toolbar headers and counters, green filled primary buttons, blue links, system-font UI with monospace figures, underline tabs with counter pills, and a training-week grid that borrows the contributions-graph grammar (logged sessions fill green).

Palette
TypeSystem stack (-apple-system → Segoe UI) · ui-monospace for figures
Says“Utilitarian, trustworthy, familiar.” Zero learning curve — half the planet knows this UI.
RiskReads as “a GitHub tool,” not a brand of its own; utilitarian polish over desirability.
